# Experience Explanation & Transparency

## Purpose

Enable Penny to faithfully explain what experience was created, why it was
created, what evidence supported it, and where uncertainty remains—without
becoming a new reasoning authority.

Foundation established accountable reasoning. Experience Explanation &
Transparency establishes accountable communication.

## Primary Design Principle

> **Progressive disclosure over one authoritative history.**

Every explanation presented by Penny is a projection of the same immutable
reasoning lineage.

The level of detail may change.

The audience may change.

The language may change.

The underlying history shall not.

## Defining Claim

> **An explanation is not generated. It is revealed.**

Penny may adapt vocabulary, structure, and presentation to the listener. She
may not adapt the facts.

The concise explanation, expanded rationale, constraint report, and provenance
navigator are different views of one authoritative history.

## Architectural Boundary

> **The explanation layer is not a source of truth. It is a lens.**

A lens may:

- magnify;
- simplify;
- translate; and
- reorganize.

A lens may not alter what passes through it.

Explanation consumes immutable reasoning artifacts. It does not generate new
reasoning artifacts. It is downstream of authoritative reasoning and upstream
of only one thing: human understanding.

> **Authority belongs to artifacts. Understanding belongs to explanations.**

## Reproducibility

> **Every explanation shall be reproducible from the same authoritative
> history.**

Interfaces may differ in:

- vocabulary;
- level of detail;
- organization;
- presentation; and
- medium.

They may not differ in facts.

Given the same reasoning lineage, every interface shall reveal the same
story—including the same uncertainty, omissions, compromises, and explanatory
limits.

## Authority

Reasoning asks:

> **What am I authorized to claim?**

Explanation asks:

> **What am I authorized to reveal?**

Neither is authorized to invent.

## Governing Rules

- Every explanatory statement shall trace to recorded evidence or a recorded
  deterministic decision.
- Additional detail reveals more of the same reasoning lineage. It does not
  create new reasoning.
- Different presentation modes may differ in expression, but they shall not
  disagree on factual content.
- Missing evidence shall remain visible rather than being replaced by plausible
  narrative.
- Recorded uncertainty shall remain recorded uncertainty.
- Alternate journeys create new reasoning lineages. They shall never be
  presented as revisions of an existing lineage.
- Natural-language generation may render explanations. It shall not author
  explanatory truth.

> **Language may be generated. Explanatory truth may not be.**

## Moral Center

When the authoritative history cannot support a claim, honesty requires
revealing the limit.

Not bridging it.

Not inferring it.

Not smoothing it over with plausible language.

The absence of evidence is itself part of the explanation.

The lens provides access, not another interpretation.

Foundation taught Penny how to reason honestly.

This phase will teach her how to speak honestly.
