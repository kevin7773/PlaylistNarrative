# Product Strategy

**Penny exists because music has always been one of humanity's most powerful
ways of changing how we think, feel, focus, celebrate, grieve, and connect. The
goal is not to maximize listening. The goal is to help people intentionally use
music to reach the experience they seek. Revenue sustains that mission; it does
not define it.**

## Retention strategy

> **Earn return visits through craftsmanship, not compulsion.**

Quality creates loyalty. Manipulation creates engagement. Penny chooses quality.
This is a product strategy: Penny should earn continued use by reliably creating
valuable soundtracks, not by optimizing for attention, dependency, or time spent
in the product.

## One Penny

Penny has one reasoning engine. There are not separate "free" and "premium"
engines. The same deterministic reasoning should construct a soundtrack from the
same objective, taste, evidence, and policy regardless of deployment model.

The governing commercial and architectural principle is:

> **Pay for capability, not quality.**

Payment may provide additional ways to use Penny, preserve continuity, or work
with others. It must not purchase better reasoning or better soundtrack quality.

## Governance principle

> **The free version is Penny. The subscription makes Penny more connected, not
> more capable of understanding you.**

This is a founding governance constraint, not merely product positioning. Future
commercial decisions must not reserve a better scoring model, journey planner,
objective interpretation, transition quality, discovery quality, or other
reasoning advantage for subscribers. Proposals that make Penny's reasoning or
soundtrack quality depend on payment are contrary to this principle, even when
they appear financially attractive.

Paid services may expand how people access, preserve, synchronize, or
collaborate with Penny. They must not create a smarter Penny.

## Deployment models

### Penny Local

Penny Local is the product. It:

- runs entirely on the user's device;
- remains local-first;
- uses deterministic reasoning;
- keeps the user's data under the user's ownership;
- connects directly to providers when technically and contractually compatible,
  and may use narrowly governed provider-documented exports when they preserve
  exact-byte authority and deterministic replay; and
- is intended to remain free.

### Penny Connected (working title)

Penny Connected is an optional service that includes everything available in
Penny Local and may add cloud-supported capabilities such as:

- profile synchronization;
- backup;
- cross-device continuity;
- household and shared profiles;
- collaborative journey planning; and
- future cloud capabilities consistent with these principles.

The cloud provides convenience, continuity, and collaboration—not better
reasoning. Penny Connected does not replace Penny Local and does not use a
higher-quality reasoning engine.

## Local-first boundary

Local-first remains a core architectural principle. Whenever practical, Penny's
essential reasoning and personal use must not require a cloud service. Cloud
functionality should be additive rather than a prerequisite for constructing an
intentional soundtrack.

This boundary preserves user agency, data ownership, deterministic replay, and
the usefulness of Penny Local independently of a subscription or networked
service.

Provider-documented exports are not a generic import feature. Each supported
export requires its own immutable source profile, representative authority,
explicit non-claims, and deterministic mapping. The initial contract is the
[Penny Local iTunes Windows XML Playlist Export Acquisition v1](penny_local_itunes_windows_xml_acquisition.md).
Arbitrary caller-created XML, CSV, M3U, folders, and invented interchange
formats gain no authority from this source-class decision. Direct integration
remains preferable whenever it can preserve the same provenance, replay, finite
universe, and legitimate-use requirements.

## Product philosophy

- There is one Penny.
- Penny Local is the product.
- Penny Connected is an optional service.
- Taste belongs to the user.
- Soundtrack quality must never depend on payment.
