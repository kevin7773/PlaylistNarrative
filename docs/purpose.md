# Penny — Purpose (Provisional)

## Status

This document is a provisional statement of purpose for Penny. Its primary
audience is future architects and contributors.

It is not constitutional authority, normative policy, empirical doctrine, or
an implementation specification. It grants no authority, defines no production
behavior, and approves no capability. It explains the purpose that Penny's
constitutional architecture is intended to protect.

The document is deliberately open to criticism. Its language should be retained
only while it remains clear, restrained, and consistent with Penny's governing
boundaries.

## Purpose

Penny's provisional purpose is:

> Penny exists to accompany people in the experiences they entrust to her
> through expressions that remain faithful to what she is legitimately able to
> understand.

This statement places the person and the entrusted experience before the form
of expression. It does not define what Penny may infer, decide, or produce.
Those permissions, if they exist, must arise elsewhere.

### Accompaniment

Accompaniment means presence without direction. It does not mean prescription,
diagnosis, optimization, promised change, or responsibility for an outcome.

Penny may offer an expression within the limited context entrusted to her. The
person retains authority over:

- what the experience means;
- whether the expression resonates;
- how, whether, or why they respond; and
- who they become.

Penny does not certify resonance. A person's acceptance, rejection, revision,
or indifference remains their own response rather than a result Penny may
authenticate.

### Entrustment

Entrustment identifies a bounded context in which accompaniment may be offered.
It is not permission to interpret the whole person, inspect an unrestricted
history, infer unstated identity, or carry conclusions into another context.

What is entrusted may be partial, ambiguous, provisional, or later withdrawn.
The fact that a person shares an experience does not make Penny an authority on
the person's life. Penny's legitimate understanding remains limited to what has
been supplied and established through separately authorized boundaries.

### Faithfulness

An expression may be faithful to Penny's legitimate understanding without
being declared authentic to the person. Faithfulness describes the bounded
correspondence between an authorized understanding and an offered expression.
It does not establish that the expression fulfills a need, represents the
person, or will cause a particular outcome.

The person, not Penny, determines whether the expression fits the lived
experience.

## Conceptual center

Two commitments organize this purpose:

1. A person is never reducible to Penny's representation of them.
2. Becoming is never Penny's jurisdiction.

The first limits every model, artifact, category, and interpretation Penny may
use. Such representations can be useful within their authority without
becoming accounts of the whole person.

The second preserves personal change, continuity, development, and future
identity outside Penny's authority. Penny may participate in a present
experience. It may not define the direction, meaning, or value of a person's
change through time.

## Four distinct concerns

This document separates four concerns that must not be allowed to authorize one
another.

### Purpose — why Penny exists

Penny exists to accompany an entrusted experience through faithful expression.
This statement supplies motivation only. It does not grant jurisdiction.

### Constitution — what authority Penny may exercise

Constitutional documents and approved contracts determine what Penny may
legitimately claim or do. This Purpose document neither grants nor expands that
authority.

### Architecture — how authority is separated and inherited

Architecture assigns responsibility to boundaries and preserves authority
through explicit lineage. This Purpose document may explain why that discipline
matters, but it defines no boundary, artifact, evidence rule, or inheritance
path.

### Expression — how accompaniment becomes perceptible

Expression is the form through which Penny may accompany an entrusted
experience. It is neither evidence of success nor authority over the person.

Keeping these concerns separate prevents an appealing purpose from becoming an
implicit permission, an architecture from becoming a theory of the person, or
an expression from becoming a claim about its effect.

## Expression is not limited to music

Music is Penny's first expressive modality, not its permanent boundary or
defining purpose.

Other modalities might include:

- story;
- spoken narrative;
- guided reflection;
- ambient sound; or
- silence.

This list is illustrative. It authorizes none of these modalities. Any future
modality would require its own approved contracts and boundaries before use.

Penny's purpose remains coherent if music is replaced entirely. The medium may
change while the purpose remains faithful accompaniment within legitimate
understanding. If the purpose depends on playlists, it describes a current
implementation rather than Penny's enduring reason for existing.

## Companion posture

Penny is framed as a companion, accompanist, fellow traveler, or presence—not
as an oracle, therapist, coach, director, or optimizer.

The companion posture places Penny beside the entrusted experience rather than
above the person. It does not imply human consciousness, mutuality, care,
expertise, or relationship merely because those words are evocative. The
metaphor describes restraint: accompaniment without authority over meaning or
Becoming.

Penny accompanies experience. Penny does not direct Becoming.

## Provisional reflection on Being and Becoming

An earlier design reflection used this progression:

```text
Being
    ↓
Relationship
    ↓
Experience
    ↓
Becoming
```

It is not a metaphysical truth, universal psychology, causal model, or source
of architectural authority. The sequence is too strong if it implies that all
experience follows one path, that relationship has one settled meaning, or that
an experience causes a particular form of personal change.

At most, the reflection offers two useful intuitions:

- the person exceeds every representation Penny can hold; and
- Penny may accompany a present experience without acquiring authority over
  personal change.

Those intuitions are already stated more precisely in the conceptual center.
The progression should be removed if it encourages claims beyond them.

## Explicit non-claims

This Purpose document:

- grants no constitutional or runtime authority;
- defines no psychological, therapeutic, spiritual, developmental, or moral
  theory;
- establishes no model of human flourishing;
- defines no evidence, vocabulary, policy, conformance, planning, provider, or
  media-selection rules;
- does not authorize Penny to infer needs, outcomes, identity, authenticity, or
  Becoming;
- does not authorize access to a person's unentrusted context or history;
- does not establish that an expression is authentic to the person;
- does not claim that an expression will cause change, fulfill a need, improve
  an experience, or produce resonance; and
- does not make a companion metaphor an empirical claim about Penny.

## Adversarial review

Every sentence in this document was reviewed against five questions.

### Does it answer why Penny exists?

The purpose statement and its account of accompaniment answer that question.
Descriptions of authority, architecture, and modality are included only to
separate them from purpose. They do not prescribe runtime behavior or policy.

### Does it claim more about the person than was entrusted and established?

Entrustment is explicitly bounded. The document rejects whole-person
interpretation, unrestricted historical scope, unstated identity claims, and
cross-context reuse of conclusions. No entrusted fragment stands for the
person.

### Does it imply Penny can direct or certify personal change?

No such authority is retained. Meaning, response, resonance, and Becoming
remain with the person. Faithful expression is not fulfillment, causality, or a
psychological outcome.

### Does it survive replacement of music?

Yes. The purpose concerns accompaniment through expression, not a particular
medium. Future modalities would still require separate authorization.

### Does poetic language conceal an authority claim?

The terms "companion," "presence," "entrustment," "faithful," and "Becoming"
all carry that risk. The document limits each term and rejects any reading that
would grant Penny human qualities, unrestricted scope, certified authenticity,
or authority over personal development.

## Remaining tensions

The following questions remain intentionally unresolved:

- Whether "entrust" overstates the relationship between a person and software,
  even when its scope is expressly bounded.
- Whether "accompany" can remain meaningful without implying human mutuality or
  prescribed behavior.
- Whether "faithful" is sufficiently distinct from authenticity when used in
  product language.
- Whether the Being-to-Becoming reflection contributes more clarity than risk.
- Whether the name Penny should ultimately identify a modality-independent
  companion or a music-first product.

These tensions are reasons for the document to remain provisional. They are not
permissions for implementations to resolve the language through behavior.

## Purpose-oriented review question

Future contributors may use this non-authoritative question when considering
the spirit of proposed work:

> Does this work help Penny accompany a bounded, entrusted experience through
> faithful expression, or does it grant Penny authority over the person, the
> experience's meaning, its outcome, or who the person should become?

This question cannot authorize a feature or substitute for constitutional,
contractual, architectural, or implementation review.

## Closing

Penny exists to accompany, not to direct.

The person entrusts a bounded experience, retains authority over its meaning
and their response, and decides whether any expression resonates.

Penny may offer only an expression faithful to what it has legitimately earned
the authority to understand. Becoming remains with the person.
