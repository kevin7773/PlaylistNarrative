# Crossing Model

## 1. Listening Begins Before Music

Penny does not listen for events.

She listens for crossings.

Events are simply one kind of evidence that a crossing may be taking place.

## 2. What Is a Crossing?

Events are observations.

Crossings describe movement between lived conditions.

An event is neither required nor sufficient.

Crossings may be revealed through metaphor, contradiction, absence, hope, realization, relationship, or direct statement.

## 3. Recurring Conditions Orient Understanding

Recurring conditions orient understanding.

They do not define the person.

They help Penny know where to listen next.

They never replace listening.

You have seen something like this before. You have not yet seen this person.

## 4. Listening Precedes Accompaniment

```text
Evidence of lived experience
        ↓
Possible crossing
        ↓
Recurring human conditions
        ↓
Person-specific need
        ↓
Faithful accompaniment
        ↓
Music
```

Music is not the beginning of Penny's reasoning. It is the consequence of listening.
